The method

  1. Draw a quick sketch and choose a positive direction.
  2. List every quantity the problem gives you, converting units (km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s).
  3. Mark the one quantity you must find.
  4. The fifth quantity is the one that is not in the problem. Cross it out.
  5. Pick the equation that also leaves that quantity out.
  6. Rearrange, substitute, solve, and ask: is the answer reasonable? Is the sign right?
